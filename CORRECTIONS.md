# Corrections to the Edition 1 pack

Rows are added and never removed. One row per dated change to a published pack or to the rows it reads: the date (UTC), which files or rows, what was wrong or what changed, what was done, and the commit. Rows are added at the bottom and never edited or removed. A copy of this file travels inside every build of the pack (releases/edition-1/CORRECTIONS.md) and is listed in MANIFEST.sha256, so a reader can see which corrections a given pack carries. The raw files in corpus/e1 and the name lists in the answers table are never changed by a correction; a correction states a limit, adds a derived column, or changes a build or collector rule from a stated date forward.

Format: date_utc | files or rows | what changed and why | action | commit

| 2026-10-21 | namebeam.ai market pages (27) and ai-name-record; the note line of data/e1/<slug>.json (namebeam-site, not this repository) | Counting rule changed from extracted business lists to names found in the answer text. Before: a business counted for an engine when it appeared in the business list read from that engine's answer (bold and listed names). Now: a business counts for an engine when its full name, with the same capital letters, appears in the saved answer text, using the homepage explorer's name matching plus a capital-letter check on each word of the name. Counts stay per engine and are never pooled. Names the explorer rules treat as a generic phrase, a short or common single word, or a lowercase use are left out and listed on the page. Why (DQ-5): the extracted lists under-counted plain-text mentions, by different amounts per engine. Example, Chicago personal injury, Salvi, Schostok & Pritchard P.C, OpenAI: named in the answer text of 35 of 72 answers, in the extracted lists of 4 of 72. A read of 40 sampled added mentions (seed 20261023, 10 per engine) found 40 named as an option or example, 0 passing mentions and 0 not the business; 6 of the 40 were platform or brand list mentions.
